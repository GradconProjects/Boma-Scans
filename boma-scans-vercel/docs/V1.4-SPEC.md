# Boma Scans 1.4 — implemented behavior and release specification

## Scope and boundaries

Boma Scans is a Bybit USDT perpetual market scanner and **advisory** trade-planning application. It does not place, amend, add margin to, or close exchange orders. Paper and manually recorded live trades remain local to this browser. No OpenAI API key is used. No signal, target, risk band or leverage setting guarantees a profitable outcome.

## Entry engine

All modes use closed candles for technical evidence and the current ticker for executable-location checks. The execution, context and macro frames are retained. A missing frame cannot silently substitute synthetic history to approve an entry.

| Setup | Trending score baseline | Weak-trend baseline |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Confirmed pullback | 66 | 74 |
| Continuation / breakout | 70 | 78 |
| CHoCH reversal | 72 | 80 |

| Flexibility | Confirmation route |
| --- | --- |
| Strict (default) | Existing planned trigger; pullback/CHoCH require their closed-candle rejection/retest conditions. |
| Balanced | Planned trigger, or a strong closed candle at baseline + 6, at least three structural confluences, two aligned frames, momentum agreement and no-chase check. |
| Flexible | Balanced route, or strong continuation/breakout at its baseline with the same confluence, alignment, momentum and no-chase checks. |

A strong candle has a body at least 55% of its range, closes beyond the preceding candle's relevant extreme, and finishes in the directional 28% of its range. The alternative timing path also requires live price within 0.35 ATR of that close and no more than 2 ATR beyond EMA21 in the trade direction. Alternative entries recalculate their stop/target payoff from their actual planned entry, rather than inheriting the old retest price.

`SETUP`, `WATCH` and `NO TRADE` are analysis states, not execution commands. `entryReady` separately requires all mandatory checks: history, score, structure, trend alignment, timing, momentum, regime, participation, liquidity/spread, funding, and target payoff. Absolute volatility halts remain active. Pullback participation floors retain the existing contraction allowance when the zone holds. Spread allowances remain style-dependent at 0.14R–0.22R.

One borderline **secondary candle-conviction** check may qualify for a Quality Override only when all required checks pass and score is at least baseline + 6. This cannot override a failed or waiting required check. The user must acknowledge the exception before paper tracking. Risk is reduced, capped at 0.5%, and the acknowledgement is saved. Multiple recorded exceptions in seven days trigger a journal-review warning.

## Ratings and risk bands

Every successfully analyzed liquid candidate keeps its raw TA score, entry grade, composite rating and relative risk band, regardless of qualification. Scores are heuristics, not calibrated win probabilities.

| Band | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| LOW | Entry ready; rating ≥84; entry quality ≥84; TRENDING; target reliability ≥65; no required failure, BTC CAUTION/REJECT or quality override. |
| MEDIUM | Rating ≥70; entry quality ≥68; not VOLATILE; no required failure or BTC REJECT. Timing may still be waiting. |
| HIGH | All remaining analyzed candidates. |

The shortlist shares the 70/68 minimums but additionally requires entry readiness, an actionable side, valid instrument rules, target reliability ≥55, at least two structural targets, weighted payoff ≥2.5R and acceptable spread-adjusted payoff. It contains at most two candidates. It is not an instruction to open both positions. Opening a plan rechecks the selected live chart and portfolio.

Scan rows expire for shortlist purposes after the smaller of 15 minutes or two execution candles. Missing exchange leverage/quantity rules prevent new paper entries. Selected last-price quotes older than 15 seconds, disconnected feeds, or demo history block new entries. A fresh chart can reject a previously qualified scan.

## BTC relationship

The engine matches exact candle timestamps, then uses up to 120 aligned closed-candle returns for rolling correlation and beta, with relative strength and residual Z-score. It never pairs unrelated timestamps by array position. Fewer than 24 matched return observations means insufficient evidence and CAUTION.

Every expanded row exposes the relationship classification and the ACCEPT / CAUTION / REJECT explanation. CAUTION can proceed only under reduced sizing and acknowledgement; it cannot be LOW risk. REJECT blocks new entries and the qualified shortlist. These rolling relationships are not permanent pair characteristics or causal predictions.

## Five-target engine

There are always five displayed slots. Candidate prices derive from closed-candle technical evidence, not fixed percentage gains. Priority is liquidity pivots and previous day/week extremes; approximate OHLCV volume-profile nodes/POC; anchored VWAP deviations; impulse-leg Fibonacci extensions (0.618, 1, 1.272, 1.618, 2); measured candle moves; then active candlestick projections including inside-bar breakouts and a documented three-drive proxy.

Each target shows price, contributing sources, R, confidence and distinct-source count. OHLCV volume bins are explicitly approximate: they are not a true traded-volume-at-price feed. Pattern labels are heuristic detections, not guarantees of professional pattern identification. ATR fallback is allowed only for outer TP4/TP5. Unsupported inner targets display **UNCONFIRMED**, are not charted as valid levels, and block new entries. It is preferable to show missing evidence than invent a price.

Tracked targets, exit weights and entry evidence are copied into a frozen snapshot. Reanalysis can alter the next plan, never the original recorded five levels. Partial-exit recording uses the saved weights and cannot count the same TP twice.

## Enter now: a separate, reduced-risk scenario

The Preferred entry / Enter now switch changes both the chart levels and analysis panel. Enter now recomputes the entry at current price, a 10-closed-candle micro-swing stop plus 0.08 ATR buffer, and a minimum 0.45 ATR noise allowance. The structural stop is never artificially tightened just to improve R.

Five close objectives use nearby swing levels and eligible full-map levels; ATR projections are reserved for the outer two. Exit allocations are 30/25/20/15/10%. The panel explains ALLOW, CAUTION or AVOID, displays estimated cost-adjusted reward, and states the actual blocking reason.

Default cost assumptions are 0.055% taker fee and 0.02% estimated slippage **per fill**, plus current spread for the round trip. Both inputs are editable under risk settings; users must match their account and instrument. Funding is excluded. TP1 must exceed estimated round-trip cost by 25%, and weighted net payoff must be at least 0.60R. This is a scenario filter, not a measured expected value. Close objectives may consequently be unavailable even when a larger preferred plan is valid.

ALLOW risk is at most 0.45% of equity; CAUTION at most 0.25%; AVOID cannot open a plan. BTC CAUTION downgrades ALLOW. Structure, momentum, liquidity, participation, crowding, volatility and freshness remain gating conditions. CAUTION requires explicit acceptance. Leverage ceilings are reduced to 70% / 45% of style ceilings respectively, without increasing stop-risk.

## Risk and trade manager

Preferred target risk remains 0.25–1% of equity. Position quantity is calculated from the stop distance, rounded down to the exchange quantity step, and checked against minimum order size/notional. Leverage adjusts reserved margin, not the quantity's chosen hard-stop risk. Style ceilings remain Scalp 10×, Day 8×, Swing 5×, Position 3×, with lower quality, volatility, stop-buffer and exchange caps applied first. This is not an exact liquidation engine; exchange risk tiers, margin mode and fees can change liquidation.

Portfolio guards retain three open positions, at most two on the same side, 2.5% aggregate open stop-risk, a three-loss review halt and the 3% daily realized-loss circuit breaker. Manual live logging records positions already opened elsewhere and may therefore record an over-limit position, but does not authorize new exposure. Paper fills are simulations, not exchange fills.

Open cards show current gross P&L, remaining allocation, suggested action, next objective, stop guidance and an expandable original snapshot. Advice does not execute itself. A stale quote suspends guidance; it is not itself a market exit instruction. Never add size to rescue a losing trade. Paper and manually recorded P&L excludes fees/funding and cannot validate profitability.

## Interface and accessibility

The charcoal/cyan command bar contains live status, equity, style, flexibility and scan controls. A collapsible navigation drawer links Scanner, Chart Focus, Trade Manager, Shortlist and Journal; mobile has a bottom navigation bar. The scanner itself starts folded, per the user's requested workflow, and its expanded contents use page scrolling rather than a nested results scroll box.

All/Low/Medium/High and Both/Long/Short filters change the view, not the analyzed universe. All liquid eligible perpetuals are scanned, removing the former 30-pair cap. Failed/queued analyses remain visible as **ungraded**, not fabricated ratings. Scanner evidence is a timestamped scan snapshot, not a WebSocket subscription to every result.

Rows expose the requested grades and metrics, expand for evidence/targets/BTC/leverage, and offer refresh-and-confirm paper entry or logging of an existing live trade. A/A+ rows receive cyan accents. New ready states have soft highlighting and toasts. Chart-first layout supports zoom, pan, deep history, unique fullscreen overlays and a five-slot target ladder. Close price/P&L ticks flash subtly. Reduced-motion preferences suppress animation.

Auto-scan intervals retain a minimum of 15 minutes. WebSocket prices can update between scans; closed-candle signals update at candle boundaries. Browser timers and background tabs can be throttled. Always-on unattended monitoring requires a separately deployed background service; the app does not claim one exists.

## Persistence, deployment and validation

Existing trade storage identity is preserved. Preferences include equity, flexibility, auto-scan state, interval and cost assumptions. The independent source contains setup instructions, tests and this specification; it requires no ChatGPT subscription after independently deploying it. Local storage is not a secure or cross-device trade database; clearing it removes records.

Release validation covers TypeScript, scoped lint, production builds, a standalone deployment dry run, and twelve regression tests including both directions across four styles/three modes, timing boundaries, cost sensitivity, timestamp alignment, stale rows, partial-exit weights, legacy storage, leverage sizing and portfolio gates. These checks are software verification, not a backtest, live-market certification or proof of trading edge. Browser/device visual acceptance and out-of-sample paper evaluation remain recommended before relying on the system.

Before any future live execution feature: add server-side authenticated order management, exact exchange risk tiers and liquidation rules, reconciled fills and positions, idempotent orders, reconciliation after restarts, audit logs, a kill switch, a secure secret store, monitoring and explicit user-controlled execution limits. None of those capabilities should be inferred from the current tracking buttons.
